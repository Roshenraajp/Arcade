2D PROJECTILE KINEMATICS REFERENCE

Equations governing parabolic motion in uniform gravitational & wind fields

1. Velocity Vector Components
v₀ₓ = v₀ · cos(θ)
v₀ᵧ = v₀ · sin(θ)

Horizontal and vertical motion act independently. At 45°, horizontal range is maximized on flat terrain.

2. Position Over Time t
Δx(t) = v₀ₓt + ½aₚt²
Δy(t) = v₀ᵧt − ½gt²

Gravity (g) accelerates downward continuously, while crosswind (aₚ) accelerates horizontally.

3. Flat-Ground Horizontal Range
R = (v₀² · sin(2θ)) / g

Complementary angles (e.g., 30° and 60°) land at the exact same flat range R! Use higher angles to clear tall walls.

4. Maximum Apex Height
Hₘₐₓ = (v₀ · sin(θ))² / (2g)

At the peak of the parabola (vᵧ = 0), thread your projectile through purple Apex Rings for bonus points.
