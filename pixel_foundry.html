Additive Light Mixing vs. Subtractive Pigment Filtering

Additive RGB Light Overlap Diagram

1. Additive Color (RGB — Screens & Shaders)

Emitted light adds wavelengths together. Starting from black (0,0,0), adding all three primaries produces White (1,1,1).

• Red + Green = Yellow
• Red + Blue = Magenta
• Green + Blue = Cyan

2. Subtractive Color (CMY — Filters & Print)

Optical filters absorb (subtract) their complementary RGB channel from incoming light:

• Cyan Gel absorbs Red (passes G + B)
• Magenta Gel absorbs Green (passes R + B)
• Yellow Gel absorbs Blue (passes R + G)
