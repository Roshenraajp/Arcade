KNIGHT'S ANGLE CODEX

1. Read the Fireball Diagram

Each fireball displays geometric lines and a golden arc marking the unknown angle x°.

  • • Right Angle (Square): Sums to 90°
  • • Straight Line: Adjacent angles sum to 180°
  • • Vertically Opposite: Opposite X-angles are equal
2. Higher Rank Theorems

Every 5 fireballs shattered promotes your Rank and unlocks new geometry diagrams:

  • • Full Turn (Point): Sums to 360°
  • • Parallel Lines (Arrows): Alternate (Z) & Corresponding (F) angles are equal; Co-interior (C) sum to 180°
  • • Triangles: Interior angles sum to 180°
3. Battle Controls & Counter-Attack
  • Click/Tap Fireball or Tab: Switch target lock
  • Digits 0–9: Dial angle degrees (no ° needed)
  • Spacebar / Enter / SHOOT: Fire arcane bolt
  • Deflecting Fireballs: Rebounds into Dragon to deplete its 1,000 HP!
